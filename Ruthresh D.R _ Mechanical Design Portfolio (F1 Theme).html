About

Design-focused, analysis-minded, and aimed at an internship in mechanical design.

I'm pursuing a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering (2023–2027) at Velammal Engineering College, Anna University, with a CGPA of 8.31 up to the 6th semester.

My work covers part and assembly design, weldments, sheet metal, DFM/DFA, meshing and structural analysis. I design with fabrication in mind: strength-to-weight, rigidity, alignment and manufacturability.

I validate designs with simulation: static structural and modal analysis in ANSYS, and CFD with Fluent. As Vice Captain of the SAE DDC Fixed-Wing UAV team, I've taken parts from CAD through fabrication, testing and redesign.

My goal is a career in mechanical design, engineering analysis, CAD, simulation and product development.
